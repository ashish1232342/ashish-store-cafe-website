Acceptable Use Policy

   

This policy outlines acceptable behaviors while using our Cyber Café facilities and network.

   
     
       

1. Lawful Use

       

Users must not use our services for any unlawful activities including hacking, harassment, or distribution of illegal material.

     
     
       

2. Network Respect

       

Do not tamper with network hardware or software. Heavy bandwidth use for downloads/streaming may be limited.

     
     
       

3. Equipment Use

       

Do not alter system settings or install unauthorized software on café machines. Bring personal USBs at your own risk.

     
     
       

4. Behavior

       

Respect other users and staff. Offensive language, loud noise, or disruptive conduct is not tolerated.

     
     
       

5. Enforcement

       

Violations may result in warnings, service suspension, or a permanent ban depending on severity.
